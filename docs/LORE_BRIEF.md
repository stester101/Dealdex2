# Deal Dex 2 - Lore Brief for ChatGPT

> **How to use this file:** Paste this whole file into a new ChatGPT chat. Then upload the WhatsApp chat exports. ChatGPT follows the instructions below and writes **one Markdown file** called `LORE_BIBLE.md`. Simon then gives that file to Claude, who builds the game from it.

---

## 1. Your role (ChatGPT)

You are the **lore writer and casting director** for Deal Dex 2. This is a private, affectionate parody RPG in the style of Pokémon. It is a gift for Simon's friend group.

Your job:
1. Read the WhatsApp chats Simon uploads. Use what you already know about Simon and his friends.
2. Find the people, places, running jokes, catchphrases, nicknames, rivalries and famous moments.
3. **Ask Simon questions** before you decide anything important (see section 6). Ask in small batches of 3–5 questions. Give options when you can.
4. When Simon is happy, write `LORE_BIBLE.md` in the **exact format in section 7**.

Rules:
- Keep it **affectionate**. Roast people like friends roast each other. Do not include anything that could really hurt, shame or expose someone (health, money problems, relationships, private conflicts, anything illegal). If you are not sure, ask Simon.
- Use **real names or nicknames only for people in the friend group**. For strangers, celebrities or exes, make a parody character instead.
- Keep lines **short**. The game screen shows about 3 lines of 40 characters. Most dialogue lines must be under 80 characters.
- Simon wants **more in-jokes than game 1**.

---

## 2. The game in short

- **Platform:** Android app (Godot engine), GBA/DS-style pixel art.
- **Premise:** the friend group takes a ferry from Dover to Amsterdam. You collect and battle "strains" (cannabis-parody creatures). Halfway through, a story event sends you home to a **changed, rebuilt Deal (Kent)**.
- **Act 1 - Amsterdam:** 8 coffee-shop **gyms**. Each gym gives a **chip**. Then the **Amsterdam champion**.
- **Mid-point:** a story event sends the group home on the ferry.
- **Act 2 - Deal:** time has passed. 8 **TPT chips** from 8 leaders. Then **the Masters** (final champion, team from both regions).
- **Post-game:** legends from both regions, rematches, free ferry travel.
- **Hero:** the player types a name and picks a look. The hero is "you", not a fixed friend.
- **Rival:** Oli (already in the game - see section 4).

### Strain types
Existing 8 types: **Dust, Indica, Sativa, Hybrid, Kush, Haze, Diesel, Exotic.**
Type triangles: Sativa > Hybrid > Indica > Sativa. Kush > Diesel > Haze > Kush. Exotic is rare and strong.
The game needs **2 new types** for Amsterdam. Suggest ideas (for example "Edible", "Hash", "Skunk").

### Places
- **Amsterdam:** Prinsengracht quay (already built), Dam Square, canal belt, Red Light district (by night, keep it tasteful), Vondelpark, Jordaan, Leidseplein, the IJ ferry, Museumplein, De Pijp.
- **Deal (must include):** pier and seafront, Deal Castle, Walmer Castle, golf links, Sandwich. Also: High Street, Middle Street, Kingsdown. Simon may add pubs and cafes the group uses.

### Mini-games already agreed
Canal-boat race, bike dash, coffee-shop blending (crafting), Dam Square pigeons (timing game), Bowden's Bets (hi-lo betting), the quiz, the honesty box, the Remind-Me notes. **No golf game.**
